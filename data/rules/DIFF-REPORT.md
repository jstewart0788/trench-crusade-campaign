## TC-002b — NewRecruit's current dataset and subfaction catalogues

NewRecruit now publishes from TrenchCrusade-NewRecruit/TrenchCrusade (`568e5abc`, gameSystem
revision 18). It turns two former Warband Variants into catalogues of their own: **Heretic Naval
Raiders** (`6497-8a39-4f5c-9873`, under the Heretic Legion) and **Procession of the Sacred
Affliction** (`c421-32f4-581e-9fb6`, under the Trench Pilgrims). The `heretic-legion/heretic-naval-raiders`
and `trench-pilgrims/procession-of-the-sacred-affliction` variants are gone upstream and are dropped here.

How a subfaction catalogue relates to its parent: it links the parent with
`importRootEntries="false"` (no parent units or variants are inherited) and Mercenaries / Campaign
Rules with `importRootEntries="true"`; it has its own root models (the Naval Raiders' Heretic Captain
is its own entry, not a link), its own `Warband Variant` entry, a mandatory (`min 1` per roster)
`<Name> Rules` Configuration entry carrying the faction rules, and root entryLinks into Mercenaries
and the parent's shared entries (Homunculus, Pile of Stuff). Its models reach the parent's
`sharedSelectionEntryGroups` (Weapons, Armour, Equipment, Fireteams) through entryLinks, and the
parent gates subfaction-only kit and prices on the `<Name> Rules` entry, whose id is the retired
variant's id. Mercenaries and Campaign Rules gate on the subfaction catalogue as primary catalogue.

Data model: a subfaction is nested in its faction's `subfactions` array (`id`, `name`, `bsId` = the
catalogue id, `keywords`, `rules`, `variants`, `unitIds`, `configuration`, `startingDucats`), never a
top-level faction. Its units carry `factionId` = the parent and `subfactionId`; their ids are
`<subfaction>/<slug>`. A roster with `subfactionId` recruits from the subfaction's list and variants
and takes the parent's patrons.

## Systemic findings

Converter defects found while diffing, fixed in `scripts/convert_newrecruit.py` instead of
writing overrides (each one explained a whole class of disagreements):

1. **`instanceOf <other model>` was never resolved.** A condition such as "hidden unless
   instanceOf Combat Medic" evaluated to *unknown* for every other unit, so the item was kept for
   everyone with a `conditionNote`. 156 spurious `equipmentAccess` rows are gone: Misericordia and
   Holy Smoke on every New Antioch unit, Tartarus Claws and Hellbound Soul Contracts across the
   Heretic Legion, Alaybozan / Assassin's Dagger / Marid Shovel / Sniper Rifle across the
   Sultanate, Goetic spells and Shovels across the Court. The same fix removes the "only if
   instanceOf Lieutenant" noise from the Sniper Priest / Trench Cleric Machine Armour rows.
2. **Dependencies were collected from already-resolved branches.** An `AND(notInstanceOf
   Combat Engineer, atLeast Eire Rangers)` group that resolved to *false* for a Combat Engineer
   still left `onlyWithout: eire-rangers` on it, so a Combat Engineer could not take a Satchel
   Charge in an Éire Rangers warband (the PDF says it can, p38). Dependencies are now only taken
   from the unresolved children of an unresolved group. 854 conditionNotes and 35 onlyWith /
   onlyWithout gates changed; the Trench Mole upgrade now correctly carries the Papal /
   Stosstruppen / Abyssinia exclusions the PDF states.
3. **Conditional cost modifiers whose condition is fully known were ignored.** The Yeoman's
   Bolt-Action Rifle is "set 5 when instanceOf Yeoman" upstream; the converter emitted 10. It now
   applies cost modifiers that resolve to true for the unit (14 resolved, 45 still unresolved
   because they depend on roster-level selections). The 10 → 5 Yeoman rifle is the reason the
   test fixtures' 338-ducat warband became 318.
4. **Upgrade limits were dropped.** Upgrades (Trench Mole, Forward Positions, Swiss Guard,
   Vanguard, Zealot Strength, Hashashin Leaf, Martyrdom Device, Studied Blade, Plague Knight
   ranks...) now carry `max`, `maxPerWarband` and `limitsText` like `equipmentAccess` rows. The
   PDF limits for all of them agree except Assassin Acolyte (Limit 3 missing upstream, overridden).
5. **Two mercenaries were invisible.** The Combat Biologist is `type="upgrade"` upstream (with a
   Unit profile) and was filtered out; the Mamluk Faris lives in `sharedSelectionEntries` and is
   only reachable through the deprecated fireteam wrapper, so New Antioch had no Mamluk at all
   and the Sultanate only had the Defenders-of-the-Iron-Wall Sipahi link (110 ducats, variant-gated)
   standing in for the 4-glory mercenary. Candidates are now any root entry with a Unit profile,
   plus models linked from a wrapper, and the plainly visible link wins over a conditional one.
   Four unit rows were added (105 units); the Sipahi price is now an unresolved note.
6. **Availability conditions the converter could not resolve were kept as unenforced notes
   (TC-003b).** "Brazen Bull only" kit is encoded upstream as `unhide if instanceOf Brazen Bull OR
   atLeast 1 Gargantuan Size (model)`; the Gargantuan Size half is a Homunculus formula no other
   unit can select, so the Flame Cannon, M.U.R.A.D. Bombard, Titan Zulfiqar, Iron Wall Kalkan and
   Grand Cannon leaked onto every Sultanate unit with `conditionNote: "only if atLeast Gargantuan
   Size"`. The same pattern put the Heavy Ballistic Shield ("Models wearing Machine Armour only",
   p25) on Yeomen, the Court's Envious Eyes loot (Misericordia, Halberd-Gun, p156) on units without
   Goetic Powers, and Knights of Avarice loot (Musket, p120) on every Heretic unit. The converter now
   classifies what a condition names: a variant (also an entry nested under one, like the Corrupt
   Merchants loot) stays a variant gate; one of the unit's own rows or upgrades becomes a same-model
   gate (`item:<item-id>` / `upgrade:<name>`, enforced by the engine on that model); anything the
   unit can never select — another unit's option, a profile it lacks, a campaign-rules item, an
   entry that only unhides "once already selected", an orphan — counts as 0 selections, so an unhide
   that can never fire drops the row and a hide that can never fire is ignored. The walk repeats
   until the set of selectable entries stops shrinking. `atLeast 0` is read as always true (it was
   treated as a dependency, which produced the Viscera Cannon's contradictory Great Hunger gates and
   the Mendelist Ammo Monk / Hellbound Soul Contract "inversions" below). Rows whose gates
   contradict each other (a Butcher Bayonet inheriting "not Great Hunger" from a Bolt-Action Rifle
   while itself requiring Great Hunger; Cradle Thrall weapons needing Dirge while the unit needs
   Great Hunger) are dropped, and one item reached through two paths is available through either.
   744 rows and upgrades were dropped (Black Grail 203, Court 226, Iron Sultanate 185, New Antioch
   66, Heretic Legion 45, Trench Pilgrims 19; listed per unit in CONVERSION-NOTES.md) and every
   `conditionNote` on an access row became a gate or went away. The same pass stops emitting the
   deprecated Fireteam wrapper units (103 units), merges same-named upgrades on a unit (`bsIds`),
   drops upstream `page` numbers (they cite an older document) and dedupes repeated limit clauses.
   Every dropped row was checked against its faction's armoury pages: the only rows the PDF lists
   that a unit lost are the Death Commando's Binoculars (ELITE only, p106 — upstream only offers
   Binoculars through Knights of Avarice loot, and the Death Commando cannot be in that variant;
   recorded under *Missing from upstream*) and nothing else; the Field Shrine rows that dropped are
   second, campaign-gated links next to the plain one every unit keeps.

Upstream data patterns that are *not* converter bugs (overrides written, pattern noted so the
upstream maintainer can be told):

- **"ELITE only" stipulations that never fire.** Upstream encodes them as a `set hidden=false`
  modifier conditioned on the ELITE category — on links that are already `hidden="false"`. In
  BattleScribe as in our data the restriction is a no-op. New Antioch Automatic Pistol and
  Reinforced Armour; Trench Pilgrims Reinforced Armour; Sultanate Halberd-Gun, Alchemist Armour,
  Reinforced Armour, Cloak of Alamut, Takwin Anqā Bird; Heretic Silenced Pistol; Court Restraining
  Muzzle ("Yoke Fiend only"). 45 of the 174 overrides remove those rows from non-ELITE units. A
  converter heuristic ("a visible link with an unhide-on-category modifier is meant to be hidden")
  would fix them all but would be guessing author intent; left as overrides.
- **`atLeast 0` conditions (corrected in TC-003b).** The Mendelist Ammo Monk, the Hellbound Soul
  Contract and the Viscera Cannon carry an `atLeast 0 <variant>` condition that the converter read
  as a dependency, which looked like inverted variant gates (Ammo Monk only under Methodius, Soul
  Contract only under Trench Ghosts, Viscera Cannon both with and without The Great Hunger). Read
  literally they are always true; the regenerated data matches the PDF (Ammo Monk for New Antioch and
  Trench Pilgrims p180, Soul Contract base armoury p106, Viscera Cannon ELITE or Dirge) and the
  overrides that patched them are gone.
- **Hidden-unless-selected links.** The Court's Misericordia / Flail/Scourge / Halberd-Gun and the
  Heretic Musket links are hidden and unhide "if at least 1 is selected": they point at the Envious
  Eyes loot (p156) and the Knights of Avarice loot (p120) respectively and are modelled as gates on
  those; the Trench Shield is hidden for models in Machine Armour, which the armoury tables do not
  say (overridden).
- **Prices from an older edition.** Court Serpent Assault Gun 45 → 50, Malebranche Sword 35 → 50,
  Black Grail Troop Flag 10 ducats → 1 glory, Court Hell Knight Unholy Relic discounted to 5
  (15 in the PDF). Kingdom of Alba Dum-Dum Bullets Limit 2 → 3.
- **Per-warband limits missing in the Sultanate.** Musical Instrument and Troop Flag are
  "Limit: 1" in every Armoury; the Sultanate links only cap them per model (16 overrides).
- **Effect keywords carried as abilities, not keywords.** NEGATE FEAR / NEGATE MINED / FEAR /
  FLYING / STRONG / TOUGH / REGENERATE 1 / IGNORE OFF-HAND WEAPON appear in `abilities` (as shared
  rules) but not in `keywords` for 11 units; the Sin Eater is tagged HERETIC instead of DEMONIC.
  The roster validator reads `keywords` (STRONG, NEGATE HEAVY), so these matter.
- **Variant-conditional prices have no slot in the schema** (Alba Great Sword/Axe 7, Alba Cold
  Steel, Cavalcade Communicant 3 glory, Naval Raiders SMG 25, Knights of Avarice Warlock 110,
  Defenders Sipahi 110, Methodius Machine Gun / SMG replacement rows). They are listed as
  unresolved rather than overridden because an override on `cost` would change the base price.
  A `variantCosts` field on `equipmentAccess` / `cost` is the obvious schema extension.

Upstream content the PDF does not have (left untouched, listed under *Missing from the PDF*):
the Red Brigade and Remnants of Byzantium (New Antioch), Children of Yggdrasil (Trench
Pilgrims), Nomads of Al-Badia and Ghazi of the Golden Path (Sultanate), Cadaver Corps (Heretic),
The Great Hunger (Black Grail), Fang of the Seething Black (Court), the Book-of-Golems
Homunculus, the Trench Dog and the Disciple of St. Roch.
