## Equipment icons

The Armoury figure's equipment is drawn from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Paths were copied
unmodified into `apps/web/src/components/figureIcons.ts`; the figure places, scales, mirrors and
recolours them, and trims the chin strap from the Brodie helmet.

  robe, spiked-mace, steel-claws, visored-helm, whip, broadsword
- **Delapouite** (http://delapouite.com): drill, flail, flamethrower, glaive, hatchet, mortar,
  primitive-torch, sawed-off-shotgun, sharp-halberd, sun-spear, templar-shield, thor-hammer,
  two-handed-sword, war-axe, warhammer
- **Skoll**: bayonet, brodie-helmet, cultist, gas-mask, lee-enfield, luger, mp-40, musket, revolver,
  spiked-bat, stahlhelm, stick-grenade, thompson-m1928, trench-body-armor, trench-knife,
  winchester-rifle

The silhouette body, the Iron Capirote and the polearms are drawn in code for this project.
